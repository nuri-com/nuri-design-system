## The walk decision (kept per the brief · now log-only · → Smell-1.1)

The brief insisted on keeping the per-component `@layer tokens` walk, its stated reason being that `token-paths.ts` derives from it. **The data flow is actually independent**: `build/token-paths.ts` derives from `classifyAll(semanticRules)` (the semantic cascade), NOT from the component walk; the walk's only remaining output is `referencedPaths` for the build log. It was kept exactly as instructed (and `token-paths.ts` is verified byte-identical); the coordinator confirmed it becomes a **Smell-1.1** follow-up.
