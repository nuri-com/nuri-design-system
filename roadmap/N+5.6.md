## Why N+5.6 not N+6.0

N+6 is reserved for Path A (Layout primitives) or Path B (Second
component). Both consume the new doc layout — a session opened
under the old monolith would write its retrospective into a HANDOFF
about to be split. Restructure-first prevents double-handling.

The X.6 pattern continues the X.5 precedent: secondary deliverable
bridging two main sessions, larger here because restructure scope
is broader than determinism (5 macro-parts vs 2 decisions).
