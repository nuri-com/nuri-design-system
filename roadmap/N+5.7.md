# Session N+5.7 · Primitive cleanup · drift removal + line-height proportional lock + font web-only polish

**Status**: closed
**Type**: bridge

## Outcome

Pure cleanup pass. Audit-confirmed drift in `styles/tokens-primitive.css`
deleted; the primitive layer now ships only declarations with at
least one consumer (direct `var()` or via an alias chain) or with an
explicit speculative reservation. A structural guardrail (test 15)
catches future drift at the test boundary.

Three layers of drift removed:

1. **Orphan font-size primitives** · 12 of 18 `--nuri-font-size-N`
   tokens had zero consumer (the type scale only consumes 6). Deleted
   the 12 orphans; the surviving 6 each carry a comment naming the
   `--nuri-type-*` consumer they feed.
2. **Line-height primitives** · all 10 `--nuri-line-height-N`
   absolute (rem) tokens deleted. The semantic layer already uses
   unitless multipliers on `--nuri-type-*-line-height` (1.27, 1.29,
   1.20, …) which is the RN-compatible shape; the primitive
   absolutes were dead AND architecturally wrong for the RN target.
   Codified as [decision 29](../decisionlog.md#29-line-height-is-unitless-proportional-not-absolute--n57).
3. **Shadow primitives** · all 20 `--nuri-shadow-*` tokens deleted
   (4 levels × 5 atomic parts each). Of the 2 docs-chrome consumers
   (`.nuri-scale-card:hover`, `.nuri-card--link:hover`), both
   refactored to border-color emphasis instead — the elevation
   foundation lands in its own session when shadow actually ships
   per ROADMAP.

Plus:

- **Status placeholders deleted** · `--nuri-color-{green,orange,red}-500`
  removed; the foundation page's Status section removed (no
  "(TBD)" placeholder — foundation pages reflect what ships, not
  what's planned). The scale + foundation section land together
  with the first status-using component (Toast / Alert /
  StatusPill).
- **`pages/principles.html:407`** premature codification of the
  shadow atomic-pattern replaced with `--nuri-color-<scale>-<step>-{light,dark}`
  — a real 4-deep naming pattern that the system actually ships.
- **Amendment 27.1** codifies font dimension as web-only —
  emulation overlay for design exploration via
  `<nuri-scope font="ios|android|pixel">`, not migrated to RN per
  the post-N+5.6 thread. Bundled edits to
  `lib/components/scope/README.md` and
  `pages/implementation-guide.html` removed `font` from the RN
  spec; the cascade table now lists data-font as a "no counterpart"
  row.

Guardrail test (test 15 in
`build/tokens-parser.test.js`): every `--nuri-*` declared in
`tokens-primitive.css` must be either (a) referenced via `var()`
outside the primitive file, possibly through an alias chain
declared in the primitive layer, or (b) explicitly speculative-
reserved by name / scale. Sanity-checked: injecting a fake
`--nuri-fake-bogus: red;` fires the test with the bogus token in
the failure message.

## Why N+5.7 not bundled with N+5.8

Two reasons:

1. **Blast radius.** N+5.8 will change every value in
   `build/tokens.ts` (cream replaces gray as default-resolved
   neutral). N+5.7 changes nothing in `tokens.ts` (the oracle
   table at `build/tokens-parser.test.js:179-244` is unmodified).
   Sequencing N+5.7 first means N+5.8 starts from a groomed
   primitive layer; merging them would entangle "are tokens.ts
   values changing because of cleanup or because of neutral
   switch?" at audit time.
2. **Pattern.** Max ~5 deliverables per session. N+5.7 has 10 ship
   items; N+5.8 adds 5 more. Combined would exceed the threshold
   `skills/close-out-session.md` implicitly enforces.
