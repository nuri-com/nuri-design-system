## palette — left byte-identical (operator-confirmed at the checkpoint)

The handoff listed palette in the fan-out set, but palette genuinely RESISTS `| Input | Web | RN | Value |`:
it is the colour FUNNEL — each role resolves to a **triple** (`Background | Foreground | Pressed`), not a
single per-target spelling, and it is ALREADY agnostic (the swatch + role + resolved hex are target-neutral ·
no "CSS" column to rename · a Web|RN split would duplicate identical resolved values). Its role-table shape IS
its honest agnostic form (the bespoke colour funnel · [§73](../decisionlog.md)); the grammar's PRINCIPLES
(agnostic · both-targets · zero prose · composite referenced) already hold. So **3 pages break byte-identity
(box · stack · interactive), not 4** — palette + typography + the 3 component pages stay byte-identical.
