## The frame — the A3/A4 carve, applied to the bench

A3 ([N+41](./N+41-prototype-carve.md)) moved the web mechanism into `@nuri/prototype`; A4 ([N+42](./N+42.md))
stood up `@nuri/doc` as a consumer package (the `package.json` shape · the `prototype`+`spec` deps · the
build-free runtime). A5 is the same move, smaller: the playground surface MOVES out of `@nuri/spec` into its
own workspace and **rewires its cross-package paths** so the DAG becomes **`playground → prototype → spec`**.
This is the carve, not the content — the rich `my-vault`-style demos stay frozen in
`@nuri/prototype/legacy/playground/` and rebuild in [convergence phase 6](./convergence.md); A5 just stands
up the package that will host them.

## The cross-package mechanism (the principled split · mirrors A3)

- **Workspace-relative `../`-paths** into the sibling `packages/{spec,prototype}/…` — the [N+41](./N+41-prototype-carve.md)
  build-free precedent (prototype's recipes + the computed-check HTMLs reach `spec` the same way · no build
  step · no import map · resolves by opening the file). Chosen over `node_modules/@nuri/*` paths: simpler,
  matches today's cross-package style, and the open-question resolution was confirmed on the rendered bench.
- DAG: **`playground → prototype → spec`** (`spec`/`prototype` never read `playground`; the "Design system"
  nav button is a cross-area HREF to the repo-root redirect, not a package dependency).
